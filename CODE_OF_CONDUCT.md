# Code of conduct

Time Capsule is a small mod for players who share worlds with friends. This page covers how we treat each other in its issues, pull requests and discussions.

## Be kind and constructive

- Be respectful to everyone, whatever their experience, age, background or identity.
- Comment on the work, not the person. Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Be patient with newcomers. For many players, this is the first bug they have ever reported.
- The maintainer decides what goes into the mod. If you disagree, say so politely and move on.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexual content or unwanted attention.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, or links to reuploaded or modified copies of the mod.
- Shaming people for how a project was made, including whether or not they used AI tools.

## Where this applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and comments), and anywhere someone speaks for the project, such as its Modrinth and CurseForge pages.

## Reporting a problem

- Use GitHub's **Report content** option in the `...` menu of any issue, pull request or comment. It goes to GitHub's moderators.
- The maintainer, [@Httpsluch](https://github.com/Httpsluch), moderates this repository and will act on what they see or are told about.

## What happens next

The maintainer may edit or hide comments, lock threads, or block someone from the repository. A first, minor problem usually gets a reminder of this page. Repeated or serious ones lead to a block.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
